A verse to begin the day

कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते
मा फलेषु कदाचन।

karmaṇy-evādhikāras te
mā phaleṣu kadācana

“You have a right to your action alone — never to its fruits.”

Bhagavad Gita · 2.47

What does the Gita say about anxiety before a big decision?
☀ Saathi
Krishna's counsel to Arjuna speaks to exactly this: act with full sincerity, but release your grip on the outcome. Anxiety grows in the gap between effort and result — the Gita gently closes that gap by pointing you back to the action that is truly yours to give.
Bhagavad Gita 2.47
